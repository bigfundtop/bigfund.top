Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about rewards, referrals, levels and claims.

What is Bigfund Affiliate Rewards?

A smart contract based rewards ecosystem where users upgrade levels, build teams and earn benefits through activity.

How do I join?

Register using a valid referrer wallet, then activate by purchasing Level 1.

How do referral rewards work?

Qualified uplines receive rewards when team members upgrade levels, based on contract eligibility rules.

What is Temp share?

Temp share is a bonus reward power that can increase your claim share during weekly royalty distribution.

How do I claim rewards?

Eligible users can claim rewards after round end directly through the dashboard.

Which wallets are supported?

MetaMask, Trust Wallet and most Web3 compatible wallets.

Is everything transparent?

Yes. Transactions and contract activity are visible on blockchain explorers.
